# Wyl Spell Recipes

# === From DnD ===

# Color Spray

*1wyl 1AP Self 1 round*

Create an illusion of a bedazzling spray of colored sand, which blind a creature.

Each creature in a 5m cone must make a physical magic save.

Physical Magic Save:  
3 wyl: The creature is blinded.  
0 wyl: No effect.

The spell ends at the end of your next turn.

*Reminder: A creature that succeeds on a saving throw to resist a spell’s effects has advantage on saving throws from that spell for 24 hours.*

# Comprehend Languages

*1wyl 1AP 200m 1hr*

You divine the translation of any spoken or written words you hear or see from any source language into a destination language you know.

There must be a creature in range who knows both the source language and the destination language. Otherwise, if the words are spoken and the source is a creature within range, or the words are written and the author is a creature within range, you get images of what the meaning is. Otherwise, you get an “unknown”.

If you try to use this spell to decode a secret message that isn’t part of a spoken or written language, you get an “unknown”.

# Illusory Creature (Silent Image)

*25wyl 1AP 20m 10min*

Create an illusion of a creature.

The creature must be size medium or large, or smaller. The creature moves relative to its spawn position.

*Reminder: You can spend 1AP to cause the creature to move to any spot. As the creature changes location, you can alter its appearance so that its movements appear natural for the creature. For example, if you create an illusion of a cat and move it, you can alter the image so that it appears to be walking.*

# Illusory Object (Silent Image)

*25wyl 1AP 20m 10min*

Create an illusion of an object.

The object must fit within a 5m cube. The object moves relative to its spawn position.

*Reminder: As an illusion, you can spend 1 AP to update its animation each turn.*

# Illusory Phenomenon (Silent Image)

*25wyl 1AP 60ft 10min*

Create an illusion of a phenomenon.

The phenomenon must fit within a 5m cube.

# Illusory Script

*3wyl 1min (10AP) Touch 10days*

Enchant a block of text to cause a creature to hallucinate a hidden message.

Trigger: A certain creature looks at the enchanted text for the first time on a turn.  
Effect: The creature hallucinates the hidden message.

The enchanted text can be on a page, on a sign, on clothing, or any other surface. Its area can be up to 1 square meter.

The hidden message covers up the enchanted text, and it must fit within the same area. Any enchanted text that is not covered up is rendered invisible.

*Reminder: As an Enchantment, If the enchanted text is destroyed, the spell ends early.*

# Mage Hand

*450wyl 1AP 10m 1min*

Create an illusion of a floating hand.

You can spend 1 AP to control the hand, making it telekinetically interact with objects. You can use the hand to manipulate an object, open an unlocked door or container, stow or retrieve an item from an open container, pour the contents out of a vial, or carry an object. You can move the hand up to 10m each time you use it, as long as it stays within 10m of you.

The hand can carry an object that weighs 10 pounds or less.

The hand can’t attack or activate magical items.

# Mage Hand Lite

*1wyl 1AP 10m 1min*

Create an illusion of a floating hand.

You can spend 1 AP to control the hand, making it telekinetically interact with objects. You can use the hand to manipulate an object, open an unlocked door or container, stow or retrieve an item from an open container, pour the contents out of a vial, or carry an object. You can move the hand up to 10m each time you use it, as long as it stays within 10m of you.

The hand can carry an object that weighs 1 ounce or less.

The hand can’t open doors or lids that open vertically, attack, or activate magical items.

# Mage Hand (Upgradable)

*1wyl 1AP 10m 1min*

Create an illusion of a floating hand.

1 AP: Make the hand telekinetically interact with objects. You can use the hand to manipulate an object, open an unlocked door or container, stow or retrieve an item from an open container, or pour the contents out of a vial. You can move the hand up to 10m each time you use it, as long as it stays within 10m of you.

50wyl, 1 AP: The hand can telekinetically move an object that weighs 10 pounds or less up to 10m, as long as it stays within 10m of you.

The hand can’t attack or activate magical items.

# Mold Earth (Excavate)

*1500wyl 1AP 10m Instant*

Telekinetically move a cubic meter of loose earth 1m away.

This movement doesn’t have enough force to cause damage.

# Mold Earth (Pattern)

*1wyl 1AP 10m 1hr*

Create an illusion of a pattern on the surface of dirt or stone.

The pattern is made of shapes and colors, and can spell out words, create images, and shape patterns.

# Mold Earth (Difficult Terrain)

*75wyl 1AP 10m Instant*

Transform a normal patch of ground into difficult terrain.

Alternatively, you can transform a patch of difficult terrain into normal ground.

The patch of ground must be 1 square meter.
