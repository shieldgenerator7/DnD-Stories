# Ransacked Golden Carriage Dilemma – GM Notes

# Intro

A traveling merchant reported that they found a ransacked carriage on the side of the road. It was gold-plaited with an eagle insignia, which you recognize as the carriage of one of the local nobles. You and your party arrive at the scene of the crime.

# Scene 1 – Crossroads

## Carriage

Obvious: The carriage is on the side of the road, clearly visible but not blocking traffic. Its wheels are sunken into the dirt, except one wheel that is off and lying on the ground.

Easy to spot:

-   Chisel and bowl of gold dust near carriage
-   Gold is completely chipped off at one side, but untouched at the other
-   Full water skin on ground
-   Giant hole blasted into side of carriage on right side

Searchable:

-   The wheels wont come out of the ground
-   the off wheel is stuck to the ground, but only at one point
-   Gravity is somehow stronger just right around the wheels
-   The carriage is NOT magically enchanted, nor are the wheels
-   The ground just around the wheels is magically enchanted to have increased gravity
-   Carriage cargo trunk completely empty
-   Carriage cargo trunk has scrape marks leading out
-   No horse reigns, or tools for animal handling
-   No sign of struggle on horse’s end
-   Carriage tracks lead off the road to its current position
-   There’s no sign of injury or struggle: no blood, no burnt flesh, no other broken stuff, no pulled hair, etc

## Campfire

Obvious: Campfire near carriage with downed trees as seats

Easy to spot:

-   Campfire was recently put out
-   Meat on stick leaning over pit
-   Stack of papers on log (carriage manifest)

## Tracks

Obvious: the tracks lead into the woods (to near the bandit camp)

Searchable:

-   The tracks are a mix of horse tracks, 1 boot track, and barefoot human tracks

## Logs

Obvious: old logs that have been downed trees for quite awhile

Searchable:

-   A patch of mushroom was recently removed
-   An eagle insignia was burned into the log

## Old Carriage

Obvious: an old carriage that looks like it could’ve been another noble’s carriage

# Scene 2 – Bandit Camp

## Tracks

Obvious: the horse tracks lead to the middle of the bandit camp

Easy to spot:

-   Its just the horse tracks, the boot track and barefoot human tracks don’t
-   The boot tracks and barefoot human tracks just disappear at one point

Searchable:

-   Where its just the horse tracks, the horse tracks look like they’ve been burned into the grass, and they look slightly different
-   Theres an ash pile on the ground, with a silver necklace in it

## Tents

Obvious: people have been living here a while

Searchable:

-   None of the manifest items are in any of the tents
-   Jars of gold dust
-   Bloody horse reins

## Wagon

Obvious: its in a bad state, about to fall apart

Easy to spot:

-   Weeds grow up around it, it hasn’t moved in a long time

# Scene 3 – Cabin in the Woods

## Ash Pile

Obvious: it’s a pile of ash

Searchable:

-   The pile is from human flesh
-   Scraps of cloth are in the pile
-   The cloth is red with gold plating
-   An eagle pin is in the pile
-   A rectangle gold piece with the name “Tally”

## Old Man

Says he lives here all alone. And he wants you to leave.

Obvious: man is covered in commoners robes

Searchable:

-   Hands are uncovered, feet are bare
-   Clothes are real, beard is illusion

## Rug

Obvious: big rug

Searchable:

-   Rug corner is curled up
-   Dust around rug is disturbed
-   Under rug is a trap door

# Antagonists

They are mages with the ability to:

-   Increase gravity around a specific area to the point you can’t lift objects that touch
-   Blast a hole in a carriage door (likely fireball)
-   Burn an insignia on a log (likely side effect of Illusory Script)
-   Pass Without Trace
